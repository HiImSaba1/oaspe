# Sprint 14 — Greek editorial content and SEO

## Objective

Publish the complete legitimate OASPE archive in natural, authoritative Greek while preserving the site's quiet editorial character. The sprint excludes the confirmed injected cluster and does not use keyword stuffing or generated doorway pages.

## Content contract

- Preserve the meaning, dates, authorship, categories and historical context of the 58 genuine WordPress posts.
- Retain the complete useful source text after sanitization; do not replace articles with short summaries.
- Improve headings, introductions and metadata only where clarity is needed, without inventing facts.
- Use vocabulary relevant to football academies, children, parents, coaches, sporting education and community naturally.
- Clearly identify historical announcements and past events so visitors are not misled.
- Keep the 1,000 injected records permanently excluded from public routes, metadata and the sitemap.

## SEO contract

- One canonical URL and unique Greek title and description per article.
- Article structured data with publication date, organization and representative image.
- Category archive links and contextual internal links to relevant OASPE pages and projects.
- Local Open Graph images only; no dependency on the legacy WordPress host.
- Sitemap coverage must reconcile exactly with the approved article dataset.
- Redirect legacy slugs only after the final slug map is reviewed.

## Gates

1. Read-only XML extraction and editorial report.
2. Human review of images, malformed slugs and the single iframe record.
3. Generate the approved static dataset.
4. Implement archive pagination, categories and article templates.
5. Verify content reconciliation, SEO metadata, structured data and responsive layout.
6. Build a new Papaki release only after all gates pass.
